# CVAdapt — Product Context

## Register
brand

## Product
SaaS that generates ATS-optimized CVs for French students and young professionals in 30 seconds. User pastes a job offer + their info → AI generates a tailored CV with ATS score, keywords, and cover letter.

## Target users
French students (grandes écoles, universities), young professionals, job seekers targeting French corporate market (CAC40, consulting, finance, engineering).

## Brand personality
Efficient, confident, clean. Not corporate-stiff but not startup-casual either. Like a well-dressed Parisian consultant who doesn't try too hard.

## Anti-references
- Garish SaaS gradients (bright blue/purple hero explosions)
- Clippy-style AI assistant vibes
- Generic tech startup aesthetic (Inter font, electric blue, rounded everything)
- Canva-looking template cards

## Design principles
1. Restraint over decoration — whitespace is a feature
2. Typography-led hierarchy — font weight and size do the work, not color
3. Professional palette — muted, sophisticated, never garish
4. French sensibility — editorial refinement, not American boldness
